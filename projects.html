2026

LendWise – Payments, Credit & Loans

2026

  • Designed a payment, credit and loan platform for a fictional digital lender: a Spring Boot system of record (JWT/JWKS identity, loans and EMI schedules, double-entry ledger, reconciliation, collections, auto-debit) and a FastAPI service for credit scoring, fraud, KYC and delinquency ranking.
